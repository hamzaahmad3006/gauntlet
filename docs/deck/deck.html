The problem

Voice agents fail on timing — and nobody measures timing

What breaks in production

  • The right answer, 1.9 s late
  • Still talking after the caller interrupts
  • Dead air that makes callers repeat themselves
  • Fine at 1 call, broken at 20

Why today's tools miss it

  • APM traces stop at the service boundary
  • Transcript evals have no timing
  • HTTP load tests have no media or conversation state
  • One developer, one quiet call is not a test
GAUNTLET2
Why voice is different

A streaming inference chain under a perceptual deadline

  • Stateful: failures appear at turn five, not turn one
  • Perceptual latency: silence → endpointing → STT → LLM → TTS → first audio
  • Continuous media: impairment shows up as jitter and loss, not a 500
  • Positive feedback: late answers make callers repeat, which makes answers later

Latency, reliability, quality and cost trade against each other — shorten endpointing and you interrupt callers; add retries and you add latency. They only mean something when measured together, in the same run, against explicit thresholds.

GAUNTLET8
Design choice

Models only where judgement is unavoidable

Uses a language model

  • The caller's words
  • Task-success judgement — verbatim citations, two passes, disagreement → needs-review

Deterministic code

  • When to speak, interrupt, hang up
  • Condition injection
  • Measurement and scoring
  • Gate and cost

A benchmark whose conditions a model chooses is not reproducible; a score a model assigns cannot be argued with.
